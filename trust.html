🔒Trust & Security

Built to be trusted with your operation.

Letting an outside partner run part of your business is a big deal. Here's exactly how we handle your data, your access, and your peace of mind — in plain language.

01Your data

You own it. We operate it under license.

PeakReach runs a managed system on top of accounts and data that stay yours. We don't hold your business hostage — and you're never locked in.

  • ✓Your data & accounts stay yours. Business data and client-controlled accounts remain under your ownership.
  • ✓Operated under license. We run and maintain the system; you keep the keys.
  • ✓No lock-in. A Standard Exit and an optional Self-Managed Conversion are defined in your agreement.
  • ✓Clear terms. 12-month initial term, then month-to-month with 30 days' written notice.
🔐

Least-privilege access

We request access only to the systems required to operate your mechanism — nothing more.

📄

Documented & auditable

Configuration, rules, and reporting are documented under PeakReach Standard™, so nothing lives only in someone's head.

🧯

Safe shutdown

If an engagement pauses, your data, logs, configuration, and opt-out controls are preserved.
