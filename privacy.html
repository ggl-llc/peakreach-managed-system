1. Who we are

PeakReach Managed Systems ("PeakReach," "we," "us," or "our") installs and manages business systems for service companies. We collect information you provide directly and information collected automatically when you use the site.

  • Information collected automatically: Basic technical and usage data such as your IP address, browser type, device information, pages viewed, and referring source, gathered through cookies and similar technologies.
