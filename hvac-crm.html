03Is this a CRM?

Yes, there's a CRM underneath. No, you don't buy software.

A CRM is a filing cabinet with reminders. It only recovers revenue if someone configures the follow-up, watches the exceptions, and fixes it when it breaks. That someone is PeakReach. You get the outcome — every estimate tracked, followed up, and accounted for — and we run the machinery: CRM, pipeline, compliant text and email, call tracking, dashboards, monitoring.
